Adds an **Atendimentos** smart button to native quotations and sales orders.
It lists Contact Center conversations for the commercial customer and their
contacts, including secondary company relationships and resolved or archived
conversations. Only the sales order's operational company is considered.

Agents may see that another inbox has a conversation with the customer. Such
rows reveal only the contact and inbox names and display **Sem acesso**. They
do not expose message content, previews, phone aliases or internal channel IDs.
Opening a conversation always rechecks sales document access, its current
customer relationship and the existing Contact Center inbox permissions.

This optional bridge depends on native Sales and the Contact Center UI. It
does not require CRM, create conversations or send messages automatically.
