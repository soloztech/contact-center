Adds an **Atendimentos** smart button to native quotations and sales orders.
It lists Contact Center conversations for the commercial customer and their
contacts, including secondary company relationships and resolved or archived
conversations. Only the sales order's operational company is considered.

Agents may see that another inbox has a conversation with the customer. Such
rows reveal only the contact and inbox names and display **Sem acesso**. They
do not expose message content, previews, phone aliases or internal channel IDs.
Opening a conversation always rechecks sales document access, its current
customer relationship and the existing Contact Center inbox permissions.

This optional bridge depends on native Sales and the Contact Center UI. It
does not require CRM, create conversations or send messages automatically.

Usage
=====

Install Contact Center Sale. An internal user needs Sales access and the
Contact Center Agent role. Existing inbox user/team grants remain authoritative.

Open a saved quotation or sales order and click **Atendimentos**. The button
is hidden when no linked conversation exists or the user lacks the agent role.
The count includes restricted conversations, independently of message count.

A single accessible conversation opens directly in the native floating chat.
Otherwise a paginated dialog shows the contact and inbox, with **Abrir conversa**
on accessible rows and **Sem acesso** on restricted rows. Selecting a row opens
the floating chat while preserving the sales form. Reopening reuses the window.

Listing does not mark messages as read. Opening follows the existing chat's read
policy. Refresh the sales form to refresh its count after relationship changes.
Merged/retired bindings and WhatsApp groups without a customer identity are not
separate customer conversations. No global record rules are widened.
