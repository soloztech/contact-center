Open the Contact Center inbox, select an authorized account and conversation,
and verify the linked contact before consulting customer records. Use the handshake
button when ``contact_center_crm`` is installed. The availability of each tab also
depends on the corresponding native module and the user's document permissions.

When a panel or action is missing, check the installed extension, account access,
linked contact and native document rights before changing configuration. For delayed
messages or missing origin evidence, inspect Base's Message Flow and attribution
records; the browser does not execute provider calls or resolve campaigns.

The `operator guide (Portuguese)
<https://github.com/soloztech/contact-center/blob/16.0/docs/crm-and-attribution.md>`_ explains the
conversation, commercial action and evidence lifecycles.
